      All controllers

      Who decides, in order

      1. Safety rules. Amber 3 s, then 2 s all-red. Green at least 10 s. Amber is never skipped.
      2. Ambulances. Lights on the route turn green about 15 s before arrival.
      3. Pedestrians. After 45 s of waiting the walk signal is next, so nobody waits over 60 s.
      4. Rush hour. The junctions share a green wave so traffic meets consecutive greens.
      5. Blind camera. After 30 s the junction switches to a simple backup rule.
      6. Otherwise the AI agent decides, checked by a coordinator that blocks obvious mistakes.

      Terms

      Normal
      Camera healthy. The AI agent controls the junction.
      Estimating
      Camera noisy, frozen or blocked. Counts come from road sensors and the junction upstream.
      Backup
      Camera blind for 30 s. A simple rule serves the longest queues.
      Average wait
      Total stopped time divided by vehicles that entered the corridor.
      BATCS-style
      Our simulated stand-in for Bengaluru's adaptive signals: actuated timing with green-wave offsets. Not the real system.
